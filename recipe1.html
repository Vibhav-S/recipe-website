Dal Tadka

Author: Vibhav Sharma | 2026

⏱ Prep Time: 10 min 🍳 Cook Time: 25 min 🟢 Difficulty: Easy 🍽 Serves: 4

Dal Tadka is one of the most widely eaten and beloved dishes in Indian mess kitchens and homes. It is made from yellow toor dal which is pressure cooked until soft and then finished with a hot ghee tadka of cumin, garlic, dried red chillies and spices. The result is a rich, flavourful and deeply comforting dish.

Dal is an essential part of every Indian thali. It provides good protein, is easy to digest and pairs well with both plain rice and fresh roti. The tadka or tempering step is what gives this dal its characteristic aroma and depth of flavour.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (yellow pigeon peas)
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 4 to 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or oil
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash the toor dal thoroughly under running water and soak for 20 minutes.
  2. Pressure cook the soaked dal with 2.5 cups of water, turmeric and salt for 3 to 4 whistles until completely soft.
  3. Mash the cooked dal lightly and keep it aside.
  4. Heat ghee in a pan and add cumin seeds. Let them splutter.
  5. Add the dried red chillies and minced garlic. Fry for 30 seconds until golden.
  6. Add the chopped onions and cook until they turn light brown.
  7. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil separates.
  8. Add red chilli powder and mix well.
  9. Pour the cooked dal into this tadka and stir everything together.
  10. Simmer for 5 minutes on low heat, adjusting the consistency with water if needed.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice or roti.

Tips

  • Use ghee instead of oil for a richer and more authentic flavour.
  • Add a squeeze of lemon juice before serving for a fresh tangy taste.
  • You can use masoor dal instead of toor dal for a slightly different texture.
