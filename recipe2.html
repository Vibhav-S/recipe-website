Aloo Sabzi

Author: Vibhav Sharma | 2026

⏱ Prep Time: 10 min 🍳 Cook Time: 20 min 🟢 Difficulty: Easy 🍽 Serves: 4

Aloo Sabzi is a simple and satisfying dry potato dish that is cooked in almost every Indian home and mess kitchen. It uses very basic everyday ingredients like onion, tomato, and common spices to create a flavourful side dish that goes well with freshly made roti or plain rice.

This sabzi is popular because potatoes are cheap, easily available and filling. It can be prepared quickly and does not require any special cooking skills. Even beginners can make a good Aloo Sabzi by following the steps carefully.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds or cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes, then cut them into small even cubes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat and add cumin or mustard seeds.
  3. Once the seeds splutter, add the sliced onions and fry until golden brown.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and mushy.
  5. Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder and coriander powder. Mix well.
  6. Add the potato cubes and mix so that the masala coats the potatoes evenly.
  7. Add a little water, cover the pan and cook on low heat for 12 to 15 minutes.
  8. Stir occasionally and check if the potatoes are cooked through with a fork.
  9. Once cooked, uncover and cook for 2 more minutes to dry out any extra moisture.
  10. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander and serve hot with roti or rice.

Tips

  • Cut the potatoes into equal size pieces so they cook evenly.
  • Adding a pinch of amchur powder at the end gives a nice tangy flavour.
  • You can also add green peas along with the potatoes for variation.
