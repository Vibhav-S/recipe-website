Khichdi

Author: Vibhav Sharma | 2026

⏱ Prep Time: 10 min 🍳 Cook Time: 20 min 🟢 Difficulty: Easy 🍽 Serves: 4

Khichdi is a classic one-pot Indian dish made by cooking rice and dal together with simple spices. It is widely regarded as the ultimate comfort food in India and is a staple meal in mess kitchens, hospitals and homes across the country.

The beauty of khichdi is its simplicity. It requires very few ingredients, cooks quickly and is gentle on the stomach. It is nutritionally balanced, providing carbohydrates from rice and protein from dal, making it a complete and wholesome meal on its own.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup yellow moong dal
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • 4 cups water
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and moong dal together thoroughly under cold running water.
  2. Soak the washed rice and dal mixture in water for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker on medium heat.
  4. Add cumin seeds and a pinch of asafoetida. Let the cumin seeds splutter.
  5. Add turmeric powder and red chilli powder and stir for a few seconds.
  6. Drain and add the soaked rice and dal mixture to the cooker.
  7. Add 4 cups of water and salt to taste. Stir everything well.
  8. Close the pressure cooker and cook for 3 to 4 whistles on medium heat.
  9. Let the pressure release naturally before opening the cooker.
  10. Stir the khichdi gently and adjust the consistency by adding hot water if needed.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander and a small spoon of ghee on top before serving.

Tips

  • Use moong dal for the lightest and most easily digestible version of khichdi.
  • Adjust water quantity depending on how thick or thin you prefer your khichdi.
  • A dollop of fresh ghee on top just before serving elevates the flavour greatly.
