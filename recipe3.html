Steamed Rice

Author: Vibhav Sharma | 2026

⏱ Prep Time: 5 min 🍳 Cook Time: 20 min 🟢 Difficulty: Easy 🍽 Serves: 4

Steamed rice is the most fundamental and commonly served dish in any Indian mess. Plain rice, when cooked correctly, is soft, fluffy and non-sticky. It serves as the base for dal, curry, sabzi or simply a spoon of ghee for a wholesome meal.

The key to making good steamed rice is the correct water to rice ratio and not over-stirring while cooking. Whether you cook it in a pressure cooker or an open pot, once you get the technique right, you will be able to make perfectly cooked rice every single time.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati or regular white rice
  • 3 to 3.5 cups water (adjust based on rice variety)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon oil or ghee (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Measure the rice and wash it thoroughly under cold running water until the water runs clear.
  2. Soak the washed rice in clean water for 20 to 30 minutes. This helps it cook evenly.
  3. Drain the soaked rice and add it to a pressure cooker or heavy bottomed pot.
  4. Add the measured water, salt and a teaspoon of oil or ghee.
  5. If using a pressure cooker, close the lid and cook for 2 whistles on medium heat.
  6. If cooking in an open pot, bring the water to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and cover.
  7. Cook on low heat for 15 to 18 minutes without lifting the lid.
  8. Once the water is fully absorbed, turn off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes.
  9. Gently fluff the rice with a fork or spoon before serving.
  10. Serve hot alongside dal, curry or sabzi.

Tips

  • Always rinse rice until the water is clear to remove excess starch.
  • Soaking rice for 20 minutes reduces cooking time and improves texture.
  • Do not stir rice frequently while it is cooking as this makes it sticky.
