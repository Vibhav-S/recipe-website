Welcome

This website is dedicated to simple, everyday Indian cooking that you would find in a typical mess or home kitchen. From hearty dal to freshly made roti, these recipes cover the basics that form the backbone of Indian meals. Good food does not have to be complicated. A well-cooked dal with plain rice or soft roti with sabzi can be one of the most satisfying and nourishing meals you can have. These recipes focus on simple techniques, basic ingredients and classic flavours that anyone can prepare. Whether you are a student living away from home or someone learning to cook for the first time, this blog will help you recreate the warm and comforting taste of home-cooked Indian food.

Dal Tadka

Dal Tadka is one of the most popular and widely eaten dishes in Indian mess kitchens. Made from yellow toor dal and finished with a hot ghee tadka, it is nutritious, flavourful and pairs perfectly with plain rice or roti.

Aloo Sabzi

Aloo Sabzi is a simple and comforting potato dish cooked with basic spices and onion. It is a staple in every Indian household and mess and goes well with roti or rice as a quick everyday meal.

Steamed Rice

Plain steamed rice is the most common staple food served in Indian mess kitchens. When cooked properly with the right water ratio, it becomes soft, fluffy and the perfect base for any curry or dal.
