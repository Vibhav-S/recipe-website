Soft Roti (Chapati)

Author: Vibhav Sharma | 2026

⏱ Prep Time: 15 min 🍳 Cook Time: 20 min 🟡 Difficulty: Medium 🍽 Serves: 4

Roti, also called chapati, is the most essential bread in an Indian meal. Made from just whole wheat flour and water, it is cooked on a hot tawa until soft and puffed up with light charred spots. A good roti should be soft, thin and slightly flexible.

Roti making is a skill that takes a little practice, especially getting the dough consistency right and rolling it into an even round shape. However, once the technique is mastered, it becomes one of the easiest and quickest things to make in the kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
  • Water as needed (approximately 3/4 cup)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon oil (optional, for softer dough)
  • Ghee or butter for applying on top after cooking

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Take the whole wheat flour in a large bowl and add salt and oil.
  2. Gradually add water in small amounts and begin kneading the dough.
  3. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until the dough is soft, smooth and not sticky.
  4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. After resting, divide the dough into equal sized balls about the size of a golf ball.
  6. Dust a rolling board with dry flour and roll each ball into a thin round circle.
  7. Heat a tawa or flat pan on medium-high heat until hot.
  8. Place the rolled roti on the hot tawa and cook for about 30 seconds until bubbles appear.
  9. Flip the roti and cook the other side for another 30 seconds.
  10. Using tongs, place the roti directly on the open flame for a few seconds until it puffs up fully.
  11. Apply ghee or butter on top and serve immediately with dal or sabzi.

Tips

  • The dough should be soft like an earlobe, not too stiff or too sticky.
  • Resting the dough is important as it makes rolling easier and gives softer rotis.
  • Apply ghee immediately after cooking to keep the rotis soft for longer.
